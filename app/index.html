Roadmap to the platinum

About 200 hours, one playthrough, no online trophies. PowerPyx rates the platinum 4/10 for difficulty — the grind is the 100% checklist, not the story. Cheats disable trophies (and saving), so leave them alone until the platinum pops.

Step 1 — Play the story 60–80 hours

Finish all five chapters. The 12 story trophies pop on their own as missions and chapters end — none can be missed. Start the optional questlines as they appear (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret) and scan vehicles and animals as you go: progress toward the 100% checklist counts from the start, even though the checklist itself only becomes visible after the story.

There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots). Quest givers who vanish for a while always come back later.

Step 2 — 100% Completion Checklist ~100 hours

After the story, open Progress, then Stats, then 100% Completion. The list is forgiving — closer to 60% true completion: most side quests are not needed, and collectibles are only partly required. The full requirements are the robberies (39 gang operations, 8 bank vaults), underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret's quests, plus any 70 of the 80 challenges, 300 stunts, 200 of 277 vehicles scanned and 100 of 255 animals studied. Nothing on it can be missed.

Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks + cleanup ~10 hours

Finish the 8 stranger mission strands that count (Kirk, Kyle, Yago and Pilot Pete are confirmed; some questlines do not count), then mop up whatever is left: the robbery trophies, the UFO easter egg, the odd one-out challenges and the miscellaneous feats. The platinum pops with the last one.

⚠ The one trophy that might be missable

Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot) is the list's only possibly missable trophy. During Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, Lucia may talk about meditating in the kitchen in the morning — follow her to the balcony and choose Accept. That first meditation becomes unavailable once Chapter 1 ends, and it is not yet confirmed whether the trophy needs it, so do it while you can. Everything else on the list can be finished after the story in free-roam.
